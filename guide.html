📖 HELLCLIMB Player Guide

Everything you need to know to climb to glory — slimjumpy

🎮 How to Play

HELLCLIMB is a chat-based board game played live on stream. Race your token from square 0 to square 100. Land EXACTLY on 100 to become the Champion of HELLCLIMB. Your name will be immortalised on the leaderboard and on the HELLCLIMB Wall — a physical board in slimjumpy's room that records every Champion forever. The board is filled with demons, super demons, cursed squares, and ladders. Use your weapons wisely.

⌨️ Player Commands

!joinEnter the game. Start at square 0 and receive your welcome weapons. One-time bonus for all players, extra weapons for Subs and VIPs.
!rollRoll the dice and move forward. 30 second cooldown between rolls. Overshoot 100 and you bounce back.
!posCheck your current square and full weapon inventory.
!boardSee the current Champion and the top 5 players closest to square 100.
!weaponsFull breakdown of all your weapons by type.
!give @userGift one of your ultimate weapons to another player in the game.
!pray50/50 gamble when you have zero standard weapons. Win = 1 free weapon. Lose = back to square 0. Once per day.
!catchCatch the Beast when it appears in chat. First player to type it wins. Must be joined to catch.
!leaveLeave the game. Rejoin anytime with !join.

⚔️ Weapons

Weapons fire automatically when you land on a demon — no command needed. Weapons carry over between streams and days until used.

🎁 !join bonus3 free weapons for joining. One time only.
⭐ Sub / VIP bonus+3 extra weapons granted every time you sub or resub. Stacks with join bonus.
💎 Cheer 101 bitsExactly 101 bits = 1 standard weapon. No limit on how many you can buy.
🎯 333 channel pointsRedeem the HELLCLIMB Weapon channel point reward for 1 standard weapon.
🗡️ Ultimate weaponEarned by cheering exactly 999 bits. Has 20 uses. Kills ALL demons including super demons. Breaks when uses run out. Can be gifted to another player with !give.

👹 Demons and Hazards

👹 Standard demonLurks on various squares. Any standard or ultimate weapon defeats it. No weapon = sent back to a lower square.
🔥 Super demon (666)Found on squares 60–99. Standard weapons are USELESS. Only a beast or ultimate weapon can defeat it. Sends you back to square 23.
👁️ Creator immunityIf you placed a super demon yourself you walk past it completely unharmed.
💀 Cursed squareNo weapon can save you. Instant teleport to square 0. Permanent on the board forever.
👹 TatoA fixed boss super demon roaming squares 25–75. When defeated he respawns somewhere new. Sends victims to square 19. Only beasts and ultimates stop him.

💎 Bit Powers

101 bits1 standard weapon added to your inventory.
333 bitsName a standard demon on the board. Include the name in your cheer message e.g. Cheer333 DemonName.
666 bitsSummon and name a super demon on a random square between 60–99. Include the name in your cheer message. Standard weapons cannot defeat it.
999 bitsForge an ultimate weapon with 20 uses. Defeats all demons including super demons.
1001 bitsPlace a cursed square at a random position on the board. No weapon can save anyone who lands on it.

👺 The Beast

Once per stream a wild Beast appears in chat at a random time between 5 minutes and 3 hours after going live. The first player to type !catch claims it. A Beast defeats one super demon automatically when you land on it — no ultimate weapon needed. Beasts are used before ultimate weapons in combat.

👑 The HELLCLIMB Wall

Every player who lands exactly on square 100 and becomes Champion of HELLCLIMB earns a permanent place on the HELLCLIMB Wall — a physical board in slimjumpy's room that records every Champion forever. Your name. Your date. Your victory. For as long as the wall stands.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

GETTING STARTED
Do I need to follow to play? Yes. You must be following slimjumpy's channel to join HELLCLIMB. Subs, VIPs and mods are always welcome.
I typed !join but nothing happened? Make sure you are following the channel. If you are already in the game it will tell you your current square instead.
Can I rejoin after leaving? Yes. Type !join again at any time. You will start back at square 0 but any weapons you already had in your inventory will still be waiting for you.
Is it free to play? Completely free. You get 3 weapons just for joining. Bits and channel points let you get more weapons and special powers but they are never required.
ROLLING AND MOVEMENT
How often can I roll? Once every 30 seconds. If you try too soon the bot will tell you how many seconds are left on your cooldown.
I rolled past 100 — what happens? You bounce back. For example if you are on square 97 and roll a 6 you land on 97 + 6 = 103, which bounces back to 100 - 3 = 97. You must land exactly on 100 to win.
What happens when I win? You become the Champion of HELLCLIMB! Your name and the date go on the leaderboard. Your position resets to 0 so you can climb again. Your name is also added to the HELLCLIMB Wall permanently.
Can I win more than once? Yes. Every time you reach square 100 you become Champion again. Each win is recorded on the Wall separately.
WEAPONS
Do I need to use !weapon or anything to fire a weapon? No. Weapons fire completely automatically the moment you land on a demon. You do not need to type anything.
Which weapon gets used first? The use order is: Ultimate weapon first, then Redeem weapons, then Bit weapons, then Follower weapons, then Sub weapons. Your most recently earned weapons are always saved for last.
Do my weapons expire? No. Standard and sub weapons carry over permanently until you use them. Bit and redeem weapons also carry over between days and streams.
What is the difference between standard and ultimate weapons? Standard weapons defeat regular demons only. Ultimate weapons defeat everything — regular demons AND super demons (666). An ultimate has 20 uses before it breaks permanently.
Can I give weapons to other players? You can gift ultimate weapons using !give @username. Standard weapons cannot be transferred.
I have no weapons — what do I do? Type !pray for a free 50/50 chance at 1 weapon. You can also cheer 101 bits, redeem 333 channel points, or wait for a sub gift. The daily safety net also gives you 1 weapon free on your first roll of the day if you have nothing.
DEMONS AND HAZARDS
A demon sent me back — did I lose my weapons? No. You only lose a weapon when one is successfully used to defeat a demon. If you had no weapons and got sent back, your weapon count stays the same (zero).
I landed on a super demon and got sent back even though I had weapons? Standard weapons cannot defeat super demons (666). You need either a Beast or an Ultimate Weapon (999 bits). Check !pos to see if you have any ultimates or beasts.
I placed a super demon — will it hurt me too? No. You have creator immunity. If you land on a super demon you placed yourself you walk past it completely unharmed. No weapon is consumed either.
What is a cursed square? A cursed square teleports you instantly to square 0 with no way to stop it. No weapon of any kind works. They are placed permanently on the board by cheering exactly 1001 bits.
Who is Tato? Tato is a permanent super demon boss who starts at a random square between 25 and 75. He cannot be renamed. When defeated by a beast or ultimate weapon he respawns at a new random square. He sends victims to square 19 instead of 23.
BITS AND CHANNEL POINTS
Do I need to be in the game to use bits? For 101 bits (weapon) you must already be joined. For 333, 666, 999 and 1001 bit powers you do not need to be in the game — except 999 bits which requires you to be joined to receive the ultimate weapon.
I cheered 333 bits — how do I name the demon? Include the name directly in your cheer message. For example type: Cheer333 MyDemonName. If you forget the name it will be called Unnamed Demon.
Can I cheer 101 bits multiple times? Yes. Every single 101 bit cheer adds 1 weapon. There is no daily or stream limit on bit weapons.
I redeemed channel points but got no weapon? Make sure you have joined the game with !join first. Also check that the redemption went through in your Twitch channel points history.
THE BEAST
When does the Beast appear? Once per stream at a completely random time between 5 minutes and 3 hours after the stream goes live. Keep an eye on chat!
I typed !catch but someone else got it? The Beast goes to the first person who types !catch after the announcement. It is first come first served — be quick!
How does the Beast work in combat? When you land on a super demon and you have a beast, it is used automatically before checking your ultimate weapons. One beast defeats one super demon. You can hold multiple beasts.
MONTHLY HEROES
When do monthly rewards get given out? Automatically on the 1st of each month at 3am UTC when the stats reset. The rewards are added to your inventory instantly — you do not need to be online.
Can the same person win multiple categories? Yes. If you are Top Chatter AND Top Bits in the same month you receive both rewards.
Does broadcaster chat count towards Top Chatter? No. Broadcaster messages are excluded from chat tracking to keep it fair for everyone.
Does gifting subs count towards Top Gifter even if the recipients do not play? Yes. All gifted subs count regardless of whether the recipient joins HELLCLIMB.
OTHER
Is HELLCLIMB running right now? If the stream is live HELLCLIMB is active unless the broadcaster has paused it. Check chat for announcements or type !board to see if the leaderboard is populated.
I think there is a bug — what do I do? Let slimjumpy know in chat. Do not spam commands as this may make things worse. The broadcaster can check your inventory and position directly.
Will my position be saved if I leave and come back another stream? Yes. Your position, weapons and inventory are all saved permanently between streams. Nothing resets unless the broadcaster runs a full game reset.
